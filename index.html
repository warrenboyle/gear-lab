What the bike is doing

    Mechanic's bench

    Limit screws set how far each derailleur can move. One click is a quarter turn. Watch the L and H stop markers in the top view.

    Front derailleur

    Gear map: every combination

    Each row is a front chainring. Each dot is one rear cog, placed by how hard it is to pedal. The rows overlap, so lots of gears can be reached two or three ways. The shaded band marks gears that feel almost the same as the one you are in. Tap any dot to shift to it.

    Things to try

    1. Shift to the small front ring and the smallest rear cog (front 1, rear 8). Look at the top view: the chain bends hard and grinds the front derailleur. Now use the gear map to find another way to get the same gear. Hint: try the big ring.
    2. Find the easiest gear for a steep hill, then the hardest gear for a fast downhill. Check how far one pedal turn takes you in each.
    3. Turn the rear L screw out 8 times, then shift to rear gear 1. Find out why mechanics care so much about that screw.
    4. Press Break my bike. Use Test all gears to find the problem, then fix it with the screws. You win when every gear runs clean.
